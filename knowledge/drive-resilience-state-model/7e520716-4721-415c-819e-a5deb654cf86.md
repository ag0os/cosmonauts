Drive finalization infrastructure failures are represented as Drive run/task outcomes (`finalization_failed`) and events, not as a new global task status and not as behavioral `Blocked`. A verified task whose driver-owned commit or task-status finalization fails remains not Done/Blocked, with recovery truth in `pending-finalization.json`, events, and `run.completion.json`.
