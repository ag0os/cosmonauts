Plan reviews make silence auditable with a coverage ledger that names every review dimension, what was checked, and either findings, explicit none, or unchecked status. When a plan wraps an external tool, reviewers should use available read-only live probes to verify exit codes, output envelopes, and claimed flags rather than trusting documentation alone; scope and size are a dedicated review dimension.
