Treat the roadmap as human-curated and agent-maintained. Humans decide priorities and add or reshape items; agents preserve operational accuracy by selecting the highest-priority ready item unless directed otherwise, moving picked-up work into the active horizon, linking its plan, and removing it after completion. This division prevents autonomous reprioritization while still integrating roadmap upkeep into execution.
